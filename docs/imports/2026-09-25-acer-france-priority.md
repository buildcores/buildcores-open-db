Its redundant legacy `type` field was removed for schema compliance; `storage_type: SSD` remains.
- CE320QKX `UM.JC0EE.X01`: official France store says black while the feed, other official regional store pages, and exact-SKU technical pages say white. Color was omitted. Conflicting brightness and unverified HDR level were also omitted.
- Webcam `HP.EXPBG.019`: retained FHD 1080p/30 fps from the feed, product heading, and exact-SKU specification table. Ignored the conflicting QHD HTML title.
- Galea 365: omitted an isolated Bluetooth 5.2 field that contradicts its wired description. Unsupported connector and microphone details were not guessed.
- Monitor response times are populated only where identified as GTG. VRB and unspecified response measurements were omitted. Generic Adaptive Sync was not relabeled as certified VESA Adaptive-Sync or FreeSync.
- Dual-screen PD monitors store the resolution and diagonal of each panel; the product names identify the dual-screen design. PE270KT stores its maximum 144 Hz DFR rate, with its 4K 72 Hz mode stated separately in the name.
- OLED brightness values use explicit peak figures where supported. HDR peak window sizes and additional source qualifications are recorded below.
- EANs, affiliate IDs, price, and stock fields have no corresponding product-schema fields and were not added to product JSON. EANs are preserved here as source identifiers.
- Human-verification timestamps were not set by this automated import.
