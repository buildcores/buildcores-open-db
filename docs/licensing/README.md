# OpenDB Licensing Guide

OpenDB uses [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](../../LICENSE.txt) for new data. The [licensing notice](../../NOTICE.md) explains which data uses this license and how to credit it.

## Community use and forks

You may copy, use, modify, and share the data for noncommercial purposes. Credit the source, keep notices, describe changes, and include the license text or link. When publicly sharing changes that need copyright or database permission, follow the ShareAlike terms in section 3(b). Private changes and your app's own code need not be published.

Noncommercial use depends on the purpose of the use (section 1(k)). Nonprofit status, free access, donations, grants, or paid staff do not alone decide whether it qualifies. Describe advertising, affiliate revenue, paid access, or business purposes when seeking permission. These examples add no restrictions to the license.

Forks keep CC-licensed data's noncommercial terms. BuildCores can license only rights it owns or has permission to license; commercial use of someone else's changes may also need their approval. The license does not give anyone ownership of ordinary product facts.

## Commercial requests

Start a [licensing inquiry](https://github.com/buildcores/buildcores-open-db/issues/new) with the product, data release, monetization, and any data, API, or fork redistribution. Keep confidential details out of public issues and arrange private follow-up with the maintainers.

A commercial license requires a written grant from an authorized rights holder. The [commercial agreement](COMMERCIAL_AGREEMENT.md) sets out the data, use, recipients, term, fees, and downstream permissions. Public-sharing duties retain attribution and ShareAlike by default. Approval alone does not waive those duties or approve recipients' commercial use; distribution, commercial sublicensing, and exceptions need explicit agreement.

The [contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md) and commercial agreement are unsigned templates. Complete the legal parties, contacts, and applicable terms before acceptance.

## Maintainer review

Before accepting new data for release under this license:

1. Verify sources, ownership, permissions, and third-party restrictions. Obtain a contributor grant where needed for commercial licensing; a PR checkbox is not acceptance.
2. Keep the exact agreement version and acceptance privately. Record the data, revision, credits, sources, and permission to license it in `NOTICE.md` before release.
3. Include `LICENSE.txt`, `NOTICE.md`, and required third-party notices in releases, exports, and mirrors. Check the delivered files.

Review is manual. Hold contributions needing commercial licensing rights until permission is verified. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#licensing-and-contributor-permissions).
